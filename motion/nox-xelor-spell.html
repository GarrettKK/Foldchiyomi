Winding

Dust and loose Wakfu drift toward the centre. The outer ring of the dial is drawn in a single counter-clockwise stroke while the gear decks start to turn.

II

The Dial

The Roman numerals light from XII backwards to I, the direction a Xelor reads time. The minute hand begins to run in reverse and picks up speed.

III

Sandglass

The hourglass forms at the pivot. Sand falls upward. Twelve threads of Wakfu spiral in from the edge of the dial and the first arcs jump from the rim.

IV

Temporal Burst

Both hands snap to twelve. Three shockwave rings leave the dial, the gears overspeed and lightning bridges every tooth to the glass.

V

Rewind
